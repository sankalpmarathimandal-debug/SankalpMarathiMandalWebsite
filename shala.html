BMM Curriculum

Curriculum approved by Bruhan Maharashtra Mandal of North America in collaboration with Bharati Vidyapeeth, Pune. Students earn BMM-BV joint certificates for the 'Marathi Bhasha' exams at each level.

Five-Level Program

A structured program from Level 1 to Level 5 covering reading, writing, and speaking. A dedicated batch for 4–6 year olds prepares the youngest learners for the levels ahead.

Schedule

The school year runs September to June, matching the regular school cycle. One 45-minute virtual class per week, plus in-person event-based activities often combined with Mandal festivals like Ganapati and Diwali.
